About Islamic dates

The Islamic day begins at sunset, so an observance generally starts on the evening before the displayed Hijri date. Some commemorations and their religious significance are understood differently across Muslim communities. Laylat al-Qadr is especially sought during the last ten nights of Ramadan; its exact night is not definitively fixed.
