How the weekly cycle works

  1. The site’s existing Verse of the Day supplies the Arabic text and selected translations.
  2. Save one snapshot for the week. The daily homepage verse can continue changing without changing this saved post.
  3. Copy the post, open the saved group, and publish manually. Mark the attempt in the log.
  4. At the next weekly cycle, generate a new verse and save it as the new week's post.

Important: this first version stores its draft and log in this browser, not in a shared database. Do not treat this page's noindex setting or an obscure URL as account security. Before adding sensitive controls or multi-device access, we should add proper Supabase authentication and server-side storage.
