How the weekly cycle works

  1. The existing Verse of the Day source supplies the Arabic text and selected translations.
  2. Save one snapshot for the week. The daily homepage verse can continue changing without changing this saved post.
  3. Copy the post, open the saved group, and publish manually. Mark the attempt in the log.
  4. At the end of the 7-day trial, review the workflow and decide whether shared storage or additional platforms are needed.

Trial limitation: the saved verse, group list, mode, and log are stored in this browser, not a shared database. Do not treat the noindex setting or an obscure URL as account security. No Glimerz credentials or social integrations are used by this feature.
