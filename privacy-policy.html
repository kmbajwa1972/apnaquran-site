Privacy Policy

How ApnaQuran handles information when you use this website.

Effective date: 10 November 2026

ApnaQuran is a free Quran reading, recitation and Islamic resources website. You can use its main features without creating an account. This policy explains the information that may be processed when you visit the site or use its optional features.

1. Information you choose to provide

Daily Quran verse subscription: If you subscribe, we receive the email address you submit. The subscription form uses Brevo to send a confirmation message (double opt-in). Your address is added to the subscription list after you confirm. You can unsubscribe using the link in a subscription email.

AI assistant: If you use the talking assistant, the text of your question and a limited amount of recent conversation context may be sent to our server-side service and Google Gemini so that a response can be generated. Do not enter passwords, financial details or other sensitive personal information into the assistant. The assistant is an optional feature.

2. Location information

Prayer-time and location features may use an approximate location lookup through the ipwho.is service to identify a city and country from your internet connection. The lookup provider may receive your IP address as part of that request.

Where supported, the site may use your device's location only if you have already granted location permission or choose to provide it through your browser. You can manage or revoke that permission in your browser settings. The site may store your selected or detected location locally in your browser to remember your last location.

3. Browser storage and preferences

ApnaQuran uses browser storage such as local storage and session storage for functional purposes, including remembering preferences, recent reading position, prayer settings and whether the assistant is minimised during a session. These features help the website work as expected. The site does not currently use advertising cookies or advertising trackers.

The website may register a service worker and use browser caching to support installation and faster loading. You can clear site data or remove the installed website through your browser settings.

4. Third-party services

Some features rely on external providers. Depending on which pages or features you use, your browser may connect to services including:

  • Brevo for newsletter confirmation and email delivery.
  • Google Gemini and Supabase for processing AI assistant requests.
  • ipwho.is for approximate IP-based location lookup.
  • Aladhan for prayer-time calculations.
  • Al Quran Cloud, jsDelivr and other Quran/Hadith data providers for selected text and resource data.
  • Google Fonts for website typography.
  • Audio hosting providers for Quran recitations and other audio content.

These providers may receive technical information such as your IP address, browser details and the requested resource when your browser connects to them. Their handling of that information is governed by their own privacy policies and terms.

5. How information is used

Information is used to provide the feature you request, deliver newsletter confirmations and messages, calculate prayer times, remember functional preferences, respond to assistant questions, maintain site security and diagnose service failures. We do not sell personal information.

6. Retention and your choices

Newsletter information is handled through Brevo and remains subject to the subscription list's settings until you unsubscribe or the list administrator removes it. Browser-stored preferences remain on your device until you clear the relevant site data. Assistant requests are processed by the service providers needed to generate a response and may be subject to their retention practices.

You can choose not to subscribe, decline optional device-location permission, avoid using the AI assistant, or clear ApnaQuran's stored browser data. You can unsubscribe from newsletter emails at any time.

7. Children's privacy

ApnaQuran is a general educational and religious resource website. It does not ask visitors to create accounts or intentionally request children's personal information. Parents or guardians should supervise children's use of optional interactive features.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when website features, service providers or applicable requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page will be revised when material changes are made.

This policy describes the website's current features and integrations. It is general privacy information and is not legal advice.
